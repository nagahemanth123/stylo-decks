04 · Across the three cycles
You ask. brAIn does the work.

brAIn is built into Stylo, so it works in all three cycles: it does the tasks, runs the numbers and writes the reports.

Cycle 1 · Material
You ask“Which lots are due for retest this week? Raise the samples.”
brAIn does
  • Lists the 4 lots due, with their dates
  • Creates the retest samples
  • Tells QC, with links to each lot
Cycle 2 · Lead to invoice
You ask“Make the invoice and e-way bill for order SO-1042.”
brAIn does
  • Makes the GST e-invoice from the order
  • Prepares the e-way bill
  • Sends both to you to approve
Cycle 3 · People
You ask“Who is due for appraisal this month? Send their KRA forms.”
brAIn does
  • Finds the 12 people due
  • Sends each manager the KRA form
  • Reminds them three days before
You approve. Every action is logged with a reason.
